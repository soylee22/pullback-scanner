04 · Method

Three conditions.
One weekly close.

01

Below trend

The weekly close sits under both the 20-week and 50-week moving averages. The prior close sits under the 14-week average.

02

Falling

The 14-week rate of change is negative on this bar or the last one. The selling is real, not a single soft week.

03

Stretched

Price closes 1.35 standard deviations below its 20-week mean for a Pullback, or 2.40 for a Pullback XL.

One per episode. After a signal, nothing new fires until price recovers to +0.25 SD above the 20-week mean. Labels lock on the Friday close and never repaint.

Tiers come from evidence. Tier 1 won at least 80% of the time a year later with a median gain of 12% or more. Tier 2 meets that bar only on XL signals. Tier 4 instruments are value traps for this method.

Backtests use Yahoo Finance adjusted weekly closes over the last ten years. Past results do not guarantee future returns. This is research, not financial advice.
