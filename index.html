Research assumptions

Entries use the next week's adjusted open. Returns are in GBP, including a 20bp round-trip haircut. FX uses weekly closes, so this is an approximation, not a fill simulation.

Outcomes use 26, 52 and 104 weeks. Adverse excursion and recovery use weekly closes, which miss intraweek lows. Unconditional baselines use every eligible weekly entry.

Ten mature one-year episodes are required for evidence tiers. Tier 1 also needs ≥80% wins, ≥12% median return and positive median edge over the same asset's unconditional entries. Tier 2 applies ≥15% to XL. These are descriptive screens, not validated probabilities.

Where evidence ends

Today's surviving watchlist is selected with hindsight. Historic replay hides future outcomes but uses subsequently revised adjusted prices. It is not a historic investible-universe reconstruction.

Correlation is not holdings overlap. Price weakness cannot identify earnings damage, debt risk or multiple compression. Verify company filings and fund documents before drawing those conclusions.

52W stretch is diagnostic only. No automatic tranche multiplier, predicted return or trading instruction is produced from an uncertain backtest.
